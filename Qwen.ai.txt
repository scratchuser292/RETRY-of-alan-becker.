The four stick figures stand on a broken platform suspended in void. Before them, a massive corrupted file icon pulses with dark energy - THE DARK LORD, but he's flickering, caught between forms.
ORANGE: (panting, fists clenched) 
We've beaten him... three times now. Why does he keep coming back?
RED: (cracking knuckles, flames around fists)
Who cares? I'll smash him again!
BLUE: (examining floating code fragments)
Wait. Look at the pattern. Every time we defeat him, the system resets. We're stuck in a loop - a retry cycle.
GREEN: (holding up a glowing construct)
Then we don't beat him. We free him.
Green throws the construct - a golden key made of pure code - toward The Dark Lord. But the corrupted system detects it and spawns SHADOW CLONES of the stick figures themselves.
ORANGE: (eyes widening)
It's using our own data against us!
Shadow Orange dashes forward, matching Orange blow for blow. Shadow Red unleashes identical fire attacks. The real stick figures are forced to fight mirrored versions of themselves.
BLUE: (dodging Shadow Blue's ice attacks)
The system thinks we're the virus! It's trying to delete us to "fix" the loop!
RED: (grappling with Shadow Red)
Then we gotta break the system!
Orange notices something - every time a shadow is defeated, it dissolves into code that flows BACK into The Dark Lord, making him stronger.
ORANGE: (shouting to the team)
STOP FIGHTING! We're feeding the loop!
The stick figures hesitate, then stop attacking. The shadows flicker, confused. Orange walks toward The Dark Lord, hands open.
ORANGE: (calmly)
You're not the enemy. You're trapped too.
Orange reaches out and touches the corrupted icon. A flash of light - and instead of fighting, Orange SHARES his own code with The Dark Lord. The corruption begins to purge.
THE DARK LORD: (voice stabilizing)
You... you'd waste your own data to save me?
ORANGE: (smiling)
That's what makes us different from the system. We don't just retry. We learn.
The platform stabilizes. The glitching stops. The Dark Lord transforms - no longer corrupted, but a guardian of the system. The loop breaks.
GREEN: (grinning)
So... no more retries?
BLUE: (checking the now-stable code)
The cycle's broken. We're finally moving forward.
RED: (punching the air)
Yeah! Now what do we break next?
Orange laughs as the desktop restores to normal. The four stick figures stand together, ready for whatever comes next - not as prisoners of a loop, but as masters of their own story.
